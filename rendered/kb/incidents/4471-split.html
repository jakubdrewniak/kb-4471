The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants whose locale writes the day first get the day and month swapped: 7 April goes out as 4 July. Every channel, every day-first tenant. Fix: parse in the tenant’s locale.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Only reports that come in through the form of a day-first tenant on booking-v3. People type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this. Fix: print the hint from the tenant’s locale.

one-day: the date moves by one day, not a swap. Tuesday shows as Monday; one meeting is Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking was made. These look like a timezone or midnight-UTC bug. The desk had already read them this way (desk-notes.json); they stay filed and counted under parser by tenant, so this row overlaps the first.

How to count

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • Count a report where the desk files it, by cause. A one-day move is its own ticket, but the desk still counts it under its cause.
  • Place a report by its tenant (locale and booking form), not by intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and gets it wrong for a tenant whose staff use another locale.
  • A subject line says how the reporter felt, not what broke. The same subject sits in all three rows.
